# Aeon QA Dataset

The main training synthetic conversional dataset for Aeon persona AI.

The data was generated by human questions and complemented by Gemini, Deepseek, Qwen, ChatGPT.

This Dataset is being created to finetune LLM/SLM's with general information about books, movies/tv and topics.

**General info**:

- General chat
- Persona validation
- Brazilian culture
- Basic portuguese
- General philosophy
- World culture
- Geopolitics
- Contemporary Art
- Basic economics and criptocurrencies
- Pop culture
- Fun facts
- Jokes
- Sense of humor
- Music preferences (Heavy Metal!)
- Science and AI
- Human biology
- Basic first-aid OTC
- Mental health
- Cooking
- Software development in theory
- Basic programming in Python
- Survival

**Movies QA**:

| Book | Genre | AI Train Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Her | Romantic Sci-Fi/Drama | **Human-AI Relationship & Emotional Intelligence:** Focuses on natural, intimate human-AI dialogue, complex emotional states, and the ethical/social acceptance of AI as a companion.|
| Terminator 2: Judgment Day | Action Sci-Fi | **AI Conflict & Sapience:** Teaches concepts of apocalyptic AI ("Skynet"), the fear of technology, action-oriented dialogue, and the nature of self-preservation in a synthetic being.|
| The Matrix | Cyberpunk Sci-Fi/Action | **Simulated Reality & Philosophy:** Explores high-level philosophical concepts (reality vs. illusion, free will), complex technical and political exposition, and large-scale world-building narratives.|
| Blade Runner | Neo-Noir/Cyberpunk Sci-Fi | **Identity & Ethics:** Focuses on existential questions, the definition of humanity, non-linear detective dialogue, and creating a dense, atmospheric, and highly detailed futuristic setting.|
| 2001: A Space Odyssey | Epic Sci-Fi/Adventure | **Cold Logic & Human-AI Power Dynamics:** Excellent source for generating extremely formal, rational, and chillingly logical dialogue (HAL 9000), space travel vocabulary, and philosophical narrative style.|
| Ex Machina | Psychological Sci-Fi/Thriller | **Turing Test & Manipulation:** Trains on psychological dialogue, subtle manipulation, the concept of a convincing synthetic consciousness, and ethical debates around AI creation.|
| The Brutalist | Epic Period Drama/Biography | **Period Drama & Formal/Business Prose:** Teaches historical context, architectural terminology, complex narratives involving politics, power dynamics, and the immigrant experience in formal, high-stakes dialogue.|
| The Substance | Body Horror/Satirical Sci-Fi | **Visceral/Grotesque Description & Social Critique:** Excellent for training on vivid, extreme descriptive language, themes of aging, body dysmorphia, and satirical social commentary (especially regarding beauty standards).|
