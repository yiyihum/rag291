# Ring-lite-distill-preview
The Ring-lite-distill-preview Dataset comprises the following components:
- [Ring-lite-distill-preview-sft-data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/inclusionAI/Ring-lite-distill-preview-sft-data): A subset of SFT data used for training [Ring-lite-distill-preview](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ring-lite-distill-preview).
- [Ring-lite-distill-preview-dpo-data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/inclusionAI/Ring-lite-distill-preview-dpo-data): A subset of DPO data used for training [Ring-lite-distill-preview](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ring-lite-distill-preview).

## Ring-lite-distill-preview-sft-data

This is a subset of the SFT data used during the fine-tuning of the [Ring-lite-distill-preview](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/Ring-lite-distill-preview) model, comprising over 2 million English and Chinese samples spanning mathematics, programming and science.
